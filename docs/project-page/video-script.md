# Demenova: 30-second demo video (script and shot list)

Rules (Space Apps, docs/spec/22): 30 s or less; English captions (Spanish voice allowed only with English subtitles);
host it publicly elsewhere (YouTube unlisted/public or Vimeo) and test the link logged out; no NASA logo, credit
"Built with NASA Earth observations" in words; no one under 18; Roberto's own voice or captions only; the former team
agronomist is not shown, named or quoted (D-027). If an AI video tool is used, add it to the SI-use description.

Honesty rules (science review T-025 and the claims review): never say Demenova "predicted" or "caught" the
22–23 Feb 2026 flood or gave lead time for it; the lots were already high risk from earlier storms. Counts only.
The demo farm is synthetic; the rain, terrain and weather are real NASA data.

Record on a phone (or Chrome DevTools at 393×852) at https://demenova-app.onrender.com/ in English.

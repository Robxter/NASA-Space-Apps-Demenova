# Demenova logo options (T-016)

Demeter (harvest) + nova (a new star). Roster picks one (class A, PENDING P-006). None uses or resembles the NASA
insignia: no blue disc, no orbit ellipse, no red vector. The star is a six-spike diffraction star, not the
four-point "AI sparkle". The wordmark is set in system semibold; outline it to paths after the pick.
Preview: `options-sheet.png` (lockups on Mist; icon at 192, maskable circle, 48 and 24 px on night blue).

1. **Core** (`option-1-core.svg`), **recommended.** A soil core in section: space above, Earth's curved limb as
   seen from orbit, banana-leaf ground, and two water lines in the root zone. It is the product's thesis, "from
   orbit to the root zone", in one object. It is also the source of the severity glyph, where water rises in the
   same core, so the logo and the alert system are one idea. It keeps its two-tone silhouette at 24 px and fills
   the maskable safe zone without redrawing.
2. **Torn leaf** (`option-2-torn-leaf.svg`). Banana leaves tear along their parallel veins in the wind. Here the
   torn strips fan out as the rays of a nova that rises from the leaf base. It is the most botanical and warm
   option, but at small sizes it reads closer to agave or palm than banana, and it says nothing about water.
3. **Nova D** (`option-3-nova-d.svg`). A heavy D monogram whose counter holds ground, a rising water line and a
   new star. It is the most "company-like" option and works as a favicon letterform, but it is a generic
   monogram move and needs the wordmark to explain itself.

Colours: Orbit `#0E2135`, Leaf `#2A6343`, water `#9ED8DE`, Starlight `#F4DF8E` on dark ground, deep starlight
`#E0B92C` on light ground. The app icons (`app/public/icons/`) come from option 1: `icon-core.svg` (full bleed;
the "any" PNGs are rounded at radius 22 %) and `icon-core-maskable.svg` (star and water at 0.82 scale inside the
80 % safe circle). Regenerate them if Roster picks another option.
